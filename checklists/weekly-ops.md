# Weekly Ops Checklist

**Owner:**  
**Week of:**  

## 1. Outcomes (15 min)

- [ ] Last week’s top 3 outcomes: hit / miss / learn
- [ ] This week’s top 3 outcomes (writable in one sentence each)
- [ ] One thing to kill or defer

## 2. Numbers that matter (10 min)

- [ ] Primary KPI vs plan
- [ ] Cash / runway check (if relevant)
- [ ] Pipeline or customer health signal

## 3. People & blockers (10 min)

- [ ] Who is blocked, and by what?
- [ ] One coaching or hiring move this week
- [ ] Decision waiting on me

## 4. External (5 min)

- [ ] Investor / board update needed?
- [ ] Customer or partner risk to surface

## Notes

_Ship the checklist by Monday noon. Don’t let it become a meeting._
