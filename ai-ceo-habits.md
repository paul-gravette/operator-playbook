# AI habits of the top CEOs

A short, sourced set of working habits from eight CEOs at the center of the AI boom. The words below are theirs. The step under each one is something a CEO or PE operating partner can try in their company this week.

Quotes are verbatim from the interviews and articles cited. An ellipsis (…) marks where a quote is trimmed at the start or the end.

## Lisa Su — CEO, AMD

> "I'm absolutely using [Microsoft] Copilot and it's one of the things that we use when you think about, you know, very easy things to do, you know, summarize meetings, track action items, you know."

On writing email with it:

> "It doesn't do it that well."

**Source:** Mark Hachman, [PCWorld](https://www.pcworld.com/article/2262634/amds-lisa-su-gets-candid-in-ai-go-slow-is-not-the-answer.html), 11 March 2024, from her conversation with Ryan Patel at SXSW. `[Microsoft]` is PCWorld's insertion.

Disclosure: Paul Gravette owns AMD stock.

**Try this in your company this week:** After your next leadership meeting, use a tool to summarize what was said and list the action items, then write the follow-up note in your own voice.

## Satya Nadella — CEO, Microsoft

> "The best way for me to consume podcasts is not to actually go listen to it but to have a conversation with the transcript on my commute using my Copilot. Who'd have thought?"

> "But it is more convenient because of the modality, the fact that I can speak to it, I can interrupt it... There's no going back."

**Source:** Todd Bishop, [GeekWire](https://www.geekwire.com/2025/the-surprising-way-that-microsoft-ceo-satya-nadella-uses-ai-to-consume-podcasts-on-his-commute/), 17 March 2025, quoting Nadella on the Minus One podcast from South Park Commons.

Disclosure: Paul Gravette owns Microsoft stock.

**Try this in your company this week:** Talk through one meeting transcript or briefing on the commute instead of playing it back, and interrupt when a point is worth keeping.

## Jensen Huang — CEO, NVIDIA

> "When I am doing something serious, I will give the same prompt to all of them, and then I ask them to, because it's research oriented, critique each other's work. Then I take the best one."

**Source:** Natasha Bernal, [WIRED](https://www.wired.com/story/jensen-huang-has-gone-nano-bananas/), 17 September 2025.

Disclosure: Paul Gravette owns NVIDIA stock.

**Try this in your company this week:** When a serious question is on the table, give the same research prompt to more than one model, ask them to critique each other's work, and keep the strongest result.

## Demis Hassabis — CEO, Google DeepMind

> "Yes. I don't use it quite as a confidant yet, although maybe at some point I will. I use it a lot for brainstorming. So, you know, project ideas, project names, you know, think creative ideas. I quite like it as a kind of sparring partner for that. And that's probably the main use."

He also uses it to:

> "…summarize some new area of research... I'm not so expert in but I want to get a quick take on, you know, the main key points,"

and later said:

> "I definitely use it as a kind of sparring partner."

**Source:** Two Minute Papers interview, ["Demis Hassabis On What AI Will Do Next"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=huAwz_BR8WM), YouTube, 25 May 2026.

Disclosure: Paul Gravette owns Alphabet stock.

**Try this in your company this week:** Use it as a sparring partner for a project name or a quick reading of an unfamiliar brief, then decide for yourself.

## Dario Amodei — CEO, Anthropic

> "I do. I have not gotten to the point where I actually allow text directly written by Claude because I just have such a specific style that I'm a little picky about it. But I basically use Claude to like, you know, to help me brainstorm, to help me think through the themes, to help me kind of, oh, you know, what are some references I could use for this? So it kind of plays a supportive role."

> "I think the thing I'm doing now does that, where I use Claude for research and I use Claude for kind of, you know, how do I help organize my own thoughts? I think if we just used it end to end, like write an essay about the risks of AI, first of all, it wouldn't write the things that I think, but also I would exactly lose that benefit."

**Source:** Bloomberg Originals, *The Circuit*, ["Inside the Mind of Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei"](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-06-17/inside-the-mind-of-anthropic-ceo-dario-amodei-video) (extended interview with Emily Chang), 17 June 2026. Also on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x2VHFgyawPE).

**Try this in your company this week:** Use it to brainstorm themes and gather references for a memo or board note, but write every final sentence yourself.

## Sundar Pichai — CEO, Google / Alphabet

In 2023, on *The New York Times* Hard Fork podcast:

> "…It's not that it's profound, but it says things and kind of sparks the imagination. In my case, it said, you should make a scrapbook. And I was like, great... It kind of oriented me a particular way."

**Source:** Richard Currie, [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/2023/04/05/google_bard_sundar_pichai), 5 April 2023, reporting Pichai's remarks from that 2023 interview (podcast published 31 March 2023).

Disclosure: Paul Gravette owns Alphabet stock.

**Try this in your company this week:** When a suggestion sparks the imagination, keep only the idea that actually orients the decision in front of you.

## Mark Zuckerberg — CEO, Meta

> "You're typing something in real time, I do this with my daughters all the time, and as you're kind of typing and entering the query, it's just generating the images as you enter the keystrokes. It's just really cool."

Of one daughter's novel:

> "…we sit and, you know, when we hang out with her, we'll use Meta AI to generate the images."

**Source:** Bloomberg Originals, *The Circuit*, ["Inside Mark Zuckerberg's AI Era"](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2024-07-23/inside-mark-zuckerberg-s-ai-era-video), 23 July 2024. Also on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YuIc4mq7zMU).

Disclosure: Paul Gravette owns Meta stock.

**Try this in your company this week:** Sit with a colleague and work a prompt in real time, watching the result take shape one line at a time, instead of sending the task away to be finished alone.


## Elon Musk — CEO, Tesla

> "Because the way that the road system is designed is for AI. It's basically, I should say, it's for intelligence, biological neural net, and eyes. That's how the whole road system is designed. So what will actually work best for the road system is artificial intelligence, digital neural nets, and cameras. And we also have the microphones. The car can hear emergency vehicles, that kind of thing."

**Source:** [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/05/20/cnbc-transcript-elon-musk-sits-down-with-cnbcs-david-faber-live-on-cnbc-today-.html), unofficial transcript of Elon Musk's interview with David Faber on *Power Lunch*, 20 May 2025.
