# Operator Playbook

Practical checklists and templates for **operators** — people who build, buy, and scale companies (not just write decks about them).

## Who this is for

- Founders and operators inside PE-backed or high-growth companies
- Investors who get hands-on with portfolio ops
- Anyone tired of vague “best practices” with no next step

## What’s inside

| Path | Use it for |
|------|------------|
| [`checklists/weekly-ops.md`](checklists/weekly-ops.md) | A tight weekly operating rhythm |
| [`checklists/diligence-lite.md`](checklists/diligence-lite.md) | Fast first-pass diligence before a deep dive |
| [`templates/decision-memo.md`](templates/decision-memo.md) | One-page decision memos your team will actually read |

## How to use

1. Copy a checklist or template into your Notion / Google Doc / repo.
2. Fill it in live with your team — don’t treat it as literature.
3. Cut anything that doesn’t change a decision this week.

## Principles

- Prefer one clear owner and a date over a long process map
- Metrics only count if someone looks at them on a cadence
- Writing is a forcing function; meetings are optional
